01 / Heavy-Duty and Dual-Use

Electrifying heavy transport and dual-use applications

A strong logistics sector, leading OEMs, and specialised suppliers give the Netherlands a strong position in the electrification of battery systems for heavy duty: road transport, maritime, aviation, and off-highway.

Where NL excels
  • Leading OEMs: DAF, Damen, VDL, Ebusco, Scania
  • Specialised suppliers and system integrators
  • Strong maritime and aviation expertise
  • Advanced charging infrastructure for heavy transport
Where NL focuses
  • Battery systems for heavy-duty vehicles
  • Dual-use applications (defence, emergency services)
  • Charging infrastructure for heavy transport
  • System integration and validation
Strategic position
Sector strength Heavy transport
Key OEMs DAF, VDL, Damen
Application Road, sea, air
Focus Dual-use systems
Heavy-duty electrification is where Dutch industry meets European climate ambition.
02 / Stationary Applications

Storing renewable energy, stabilising the grid

Renewable energy generation, experience with grid congestion solutions, and strong system integration give the Netherlands a distinctive position in short- and long-duration stationary energy storage.

Where NL excels
  • Grid congestion solutions and management
  • System integration expertise
  • Renewable energy infrastructure
  • Energy storage innovation (Aquabattery, Battolyser, Elestor)
Where NL focuses
  • Short- and long-duration energy storage
  • Grid-scale battery systems
  • Integration with renewable generation
  • Bi-directional charging technology
Strategic position
Sector strength Grid integration
Key innovators Elestor, Aquabattery
Duration Short + long-term
Focus Grid stability
From congestion management to long-duration storage: the grid of tomorrow starts here.
03 / Next-Gen Components and Materials

Pioneering the materials that power tomorrow's batteries

Extensive experience with material innovation and production technology gives the Netherlands a strong starting position in next-generation battery materials, cell components, and production technology.

Where NL excels
  • Silicon anode technology (LeydenJar)
  • Solid-state batteries (LionVolt)
  • Thin-film and atomic layer deposition (SALD, SparkNano)
  • Bulk-type batteries (Aquabattery, Battolyser)
  • Advanced materials research (TU Delft, TU/e, RUG)
Where NL focuses
  • Alternative and sustainable battery materials
  • Scaling of manufacturing equipment for cell components
  • R&D and pilot facilities for pre-industrialisation
  • High energy density and long cycle life
Strategic position
Sector strength Materials science
Key innovators LeydenJar, LionVolt
Research TU Delft, TU/e, RUG
Focus Next-gen materials
From silicon anodes to solid-state: Dutch materials science powers the next generation.
04 / Production Equipment and Circularity

Building the machines, closing the loop

The Netherlands' internationally competitive high-tech manufacturing industry offers a strong starting position for battery production equipment. As European leader in circular material use, the Netherlands combines a favourable geographic position with expertise in diagnostics, life extension, and recycling.

Production equipment
  • High-tech manufacturing and mechatronics
  • Systems engineering and precision equipment
  • Automation of assembly lines
  • Scaling and industrialisation support
Circular value chain
  • Diagnostics and life extension
  • High-quality recycling (up to 98% recovery)
  • Material recovery and refining
  • Second-use battery systems
  • Digital battery passport development
Strategic position
Sector strength High-tech mfg
Recycling rate Up to 98%
Key partners ARN, Back to Battery
Focus Circular systems
From production equipment to circular recovery: the full value chain, made in the Netherlands.
